# Bixi Favicon Redesign

## Objective

Create a more abstract, distinctive Bixi favicon that remains recognizable at 16, 32, and 64 pixels. The first exploration will compare two brand routes: an abstract turtle-shell mark and an abstract tourmaline/gemstone mark.

## Design principles

- No text, wordmark, facial details, or illustrative clutter.
- Use a bold silhouette and one memorable negative-space gesture.
- Limit each mark to two or three flat colors with strong contrast.
- Keep the mark legible on both light and dark browser chrome.
- Preserve a restrained connection to Bixi's existing blue, green, and coral palette without copying the current multi-petal icon.
- Design for square favicon framing with safe padding and no dependence on a surrounding circular container.

## Exploration set

### Route 1: Abstract turtle shell + B

- **1A — Shell B:** a compact six-segment shell silhouette with a geometric negative-space `B` gesture through the center.
- **1B — Fortified shell:** a rounded shield/shell hybrid that communicates stability and platform foundation; the shell divisions remain minimal.
- **1C — Shell facet:** an extremely reduced shell made from a few angular facets, retaining only enough structure to suggest a turtle back without drawing a complete turtle.

### Route 2: Abstract tourmaline gemstone

- **2A — Faceted B gem:** a compact tourmaline crystal silhouette with a central geometric `B` cutout.
- **2B — Diamond/V cut:** a rhombus gemstone with a strong V-shaped internal cut, preserving a visual echo of the current mark while simplifying it.
- **2C — Gem seal:** a blue-green geometric gemstone seal with a restrained coral highlight and a single dominant silhouette.

## Common output constraints

- Square 1:1 composition.
- Flat vector-like raster treatment suitable for favicon export.
- No gradients, shadows, texture, glow, watermark, or background scene.
- Avoid thin interior lines that disappear below 32 pixels.
- Generate six visual drafts first; select one direction before replacing project assets.

## Selection and implementation

The six drafts are for comparison only. After selection, the chosen mark will be refined at favicon sizes, exported to PNG and ICO as needed, and wired to the existing `/favicon.ico` reference. Existing assets remain untouched until a final mark is approved.
